The count

Officers can shave a year off the sentence. The yard can put one back on. An administration job takes a year away and costs 2 respect. An inmate job adds a year and pays 2 respect. Respect never rises above 100.

Help the block too often and the officers stop listening. Serve the office too often and the yard stops trusting you. You are not escaping. You are deciding what the century is worth.

Administration

Scrub a bowl. Mop an aisle. File a slip. Carry a name back to the warden. The book gets lighter. The yard likes you less.

−1 year · −2 respect

The yard

Pass a word. Find a letter. Do the reps. Keep a bench quiet. The sentence grows, and so does your name in here.

+1 year · +2 respect

Inside the wire

The prison is seen from above, as if the roofs were lifted off. The camera stays with Kane. A thumbstick sits on the left. The action button sits on the right. Get close before anyone will talk. A card opens with their name and face. People with nothing for you still answer. Most of them tell you to keep walking.

Quests are errands. Someone sends you to a place or to another person. A faint green arrow points the way, even through walls. Do the job, walk back, and report. The book updates, and the phone saves. The same school of errands comes around again.

On the phone

The game opens in landscape and plays on the device. There is no account and no server. New Game asks before it burns the old save. Continue picks up after the last finished job. Volume runs from 0 to 100. Menus can be English, Spanish, German, Hindi, Japanese, or Chinese. The story stays in English.

Package com.hundredyears.freedom. A quiet score under the menu, another under the yard. Footsteps, doors, and talk stay soft.
