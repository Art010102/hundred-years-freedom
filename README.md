# A Hundred Years to Freedom

![A Hundred Years to Freedom](banner.jpg)

Ellis Kane, inmate 104, starts with a hundred years. Officers can shave a year off. The yard can put one back on. Respect never rises above 100. You are not escaping. You are deciding what the century is worth.

**Package:** `com.hundredyears.freedom`

[Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.hundredyears.freedom) · [Privacy policy](PRIVACY.md)

## The count

You walk a brick block, a guarded corridor, a yard, a mess hall, and the showers. The camera stays above you. Talk only when you are close enough to touch someone. Everyone has something to say, even if it is only “keep walking.”

Twenty errands loop. An officer’s job takes one year off the sentence and costs two respect. A job from another inmate adds one year and pays two respect. Nothing in the block is a weapon, and there is no escape to win.

## On the phone

The Android app is offline. Progress stays on the device: years left, respect, and which errand you are on. Cell doors open when you press the action button, then shut themselves after two seconds.
