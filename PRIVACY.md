# Privacy policy

**A Hundred Years to Freedom.** Last updated 23 September 2026.

[Back to the site](https://github.com/Art010102/hundred-years-freedom)

## What this is

A Hundred Years to Freedom is a single-player game about inmate 104 working errands inside a fictional prison. It is not a management sim, not a multiplayer service, and not an account system. The Android package id is `com.hundredyears.freedom`.

## What stays on your device

The game writes one small save on the phone, and only there:

- years left on the sentence
- respect, from 0 to 100
- which errand in the loop is active, and whether you are going or reporting back
- the name of an item you are carrying, if any

That save is local storage. Uninstalling the app, or clearing the app’s storage, deletes it. We do not keep a copy.

## What we do not take

- No account, name, email, or phone number from you inside the game
- No location, contacts, photos, camera, or microphone
- No advertising identifier and no ad network
- No analytics, crash-upload service, or third-party tracker
- No payment data. The game has no store inside it
- Nothing is sold, rented, or shared, because nothing leaves the device

The Android shell may declare an internet permission because the game view is a packaged web page. The game itself does not open a connection to us or to anyone else in order to play. Google Play’s own services, if you install from the store, are Google’s, not this game’s.

## Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and it does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.
